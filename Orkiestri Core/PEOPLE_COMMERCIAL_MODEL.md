# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the commercial strategy for People Hub.

The objective is to establish:

- Product positioning.
- Packaging strategy.
- Licensing model.
- Revenue opportunities.
- Expansion strategy.

---

# 2. PRODUCT POSITIONING

People Hub is an enterprise Human Resources and Personnel Administration platform inside the Orkiestri ecosystem.

The product combines:

- Employee management.
- Human resources processes.
- Personnel administration.
- Workflow automation.
- Artificial Intelligence.
- Business intelligence.

---

# 3. COMMERCIAL MODEL PRINCIPLE

People Hub follows a modular SaaS strategy.

Customers may:

- Use People Hub as part of Orkiestri HUB.
- Purchase People Hub as an independent solution.
- Expand with additional Orkiestri modules.

# 6. PRODUCT PACKAGING

## PEOPLE CORE

Purpose:

Provide essential employee management.

Includes:

- Employee registration.
- Employee profile.
- Organizational structure.
- Employee documents.
- Basic reports.

Target:

Small companies.

---

## PEOPLE BUSINESS

Purpose:

Provide complete HR operational management.

Includes:

Everything from People Core.

Additional:

- Employee requests.
- Approval workflows.
- Vacation management.
- Benefits management.
- Advanced permissions.
- Notifications.

Target:

Medium companies.

---

## PEOPLE ENTERPRISE

Purpose:

Provide strategic people intelligence.

Includes:

Everything from People Business.

Additional:

- AI assistant.
- Advanced analytics.
- Custom workflows.
- Enterprise integrations.
- Advanced security.
- Dedicated support.

Target:

Large organizations.
